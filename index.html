How I Coach Administrators

The same cycle great teachers use, applied to leaders.

01

Observe

Start where the program actually is. Sit in on the workflow, watch how data gets entered and used, and listen for the friction leaders may not name themselves.

02

Document

Turn what I see into a clear picture: adoption gaps, usage patterns, and the two or three levers that will move program quality fastest.

03

Plan Together

Build a success plan with the administrator, not for them. Concrete goals, leadership rhythms, and accountability cadences they can sustain after I leave the room.

04

Follow Through

Coaching is a cycle, not an event. Regular check-ins, celebrated wins, and course corrections until the platform is simply how the program runs.

If this cycle looks familiar, it should. It is how early childhood educators are taught to support children. Administrators deserve the same intentional support.
