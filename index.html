A little about my journey

I know the work because I've done the work.

“My mission is to make sure teachers and coaches can make a lasting impact on students’ futures through high-quality teaching and learning experiences.”

My career began in early childhood classrooms, where I learned that meaningful teaching depends on relationships, intentional practice, and a clear understanding of how children grow. I used Teaching Strategies GOLD to observe and document development and The Creative Curriculum to plan responsive learning experiences.

I then moved into instructional coaching, partnering with educators and site leaders to strengthen classroom practice, use assessment information thoughtfully, and create professional learning that translates into action. At Smart Start of Mecklenburg County, I supported educators and directors across multiple sites through coaching, PLCs, observation, feedback, and curriculum support.

Today, I bring that educator-first lens to my work as a Customer Success Manager at Imagine Learning. I support K–12 district partners through onboarding, training, success planning, adoption insights, and cross-functional problem-solving. The common thread has never changed: listen first, make the complex manageable, and help people feel confident using the tools and practices that move learning forward.

How I coach

Support that meets people where they are—and helps them move forward.

Strong coaching is intentional, collaborative, and grounded in evidence. I create space for reflection while keeping the next step practical and achievable.

01 / LISTEN

Build trust first

Understand the educator’s context, strengths, goals, and barriers before recommending a solution.

02 / NOTICE

Use evidence well

Connect observation, assessment, and engagement data to what is happening in daily practice.

03 / LEARN

Make learning relevant

Facilitate professional learning that is engaging, accessible, and connected to real classroom needs.

04 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Turn insight into action

Agree on a manageable next step, revisit progress, celebrate growth, and adjust together.

When we strengthen coaches, we strengthen teachers—and ultimately, we create better outcomes for children.
